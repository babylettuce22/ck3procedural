# The march: a settled county that borders unsettled wilds or a ruin.
#
# No upside on purpose. The wild next door is a problem to be solved, by colonising it or by
# rebuilding the ruin, and the modifier lifts the moment that happens (gen_march_refresh_*, in
# common/scripted_effects/00_march_effects.txt). Development growth is the real cost: it compounds
# over decades, while the tax and opinion terms are what make it visible on day one.
#
# Sized against vanilla's own steps (development growth +-0.10..0.20, tax +-0.05..0.10, travel
# danger +-5..10) and the clearing modifiers beside this file.
#
# Control, CK3 1.21: a county drifts toward a baseline (35) at only 0.1 a month, and vanilla's
# penalties lower that baseline (control_baseline_add -5/-10/-15), never the monthly rate. The 1.20
# port kept -0.2/-0.3 a month here, which outran the 0.1 drift and pinned march counties at zero
# control for good (measured 2026-11-19, a 115-year 1.21 game: 31-34% of march counties under 10,
# against 11% elsewhere) and fed almost every ruin. So the march lowers where the county settles,
# 25 and 20, and leaves it recovering toward that. The two are
# exclusive: a county carries one or the other, never both. Measured 2026-10-05 on seven worlds: 4-10%
# of settled counties border the wild, about a third of those border two or more wild counties.

# One wild neighbour.
gen_wild_march_modifier = {
	icon = outdoors_negative
	prosperity_growth_factor = -0.15
	control_baseline_add = -10
	all_tax_mult = -0.05
	travel_danger = 5
	popular_opinion_add = -5
}
